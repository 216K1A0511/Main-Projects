# 5. HOD (Head of Department) Portal Working Process

## Overview
The **HOD Portal** carries supervisory logic over specific academic departments (like Computer Science, Mechanical). They oversee both the Students mapped to them and the Faculty operating under them.

## Working Process & Files involved

1. **`hod-dashboard.html`**
   - **Role:** Departmental macro-view.
   - **Process:** Quick snapshot of department performance (average GPA, attendance dips), faculty compliance (who hasn't uploaded marks), and department specific events.

2. **`hod-academic-setup.html`**
   - **Role:** Supervisor workflows.
   - **Process:** 
     - Assigns specific subjects to specific faculties (Workload allocation).
     - Approves/Rejects the Lesson Plans created by their faculties.
     - Reviews and publishes final grades to the students.
     - Monitors overall syllabus completion progress across all batches inside their department.
