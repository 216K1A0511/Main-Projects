# 9. Placement Coordinator Portal Working Process

## Overview
The **Placement Portal** serves to bridge students transitioning into professional environments, managing internal up-skilling (training) and external recruitment drives.

## Working Process & Files involved

1. **`placement-coordinator-dashboard.html`**
   - **Role:** Career pipeline logic.
   - **Process:** 
     - **Drive Creation:** Designing the physical "Event" structure for upcoming corporate recruitment drives, including defining thresholds (E.g., cutoff 7.0 GPA) which implicitly filter students in `student-dashboard.html`.
     - **Application Processing:** The coordinator reviews the batches of uploaded resumes generated by Student opt-ins, managing their interview rounds locally (Aptitude, Tech Interview, HR Round).
     - **Training Logic:** Sets up upcoming seminars and tracks student completion/attendance purely related to soft-skills and coding bootcamp trainings outside of core academics.
