# 4. Faculty / Employee Portal Working Process

## Overview
The **Faculty/Employee Portal** acts as the primary driver of academic inputs. Faculties dictate class schedules, notes, marks, and attendance which ripple outwards to students and admins.

## Working Process & Files involved

1. **`employee-dashboard.html`**
   - **Role:** Daily agenda overview.
   - **Process:** Features the upcoming daily lecture schedule, a Quick-Mark Attendance portal, outstanding grading notifications, and employee-specific announcements (like salary release).

2. **`employee-academic-setup.html` & `academic-module.html`**
   - **Role:** Course Management Hub.
   - **Process:** 
     - Instructors create their Lesson Plans here.
     - They upload resources/assignments and configure deadlines.
     - They log detailed student grades for mid-semesters and practicals.
     - They mark daily student attendance.

3. **`employee-resignation.html`**
   - **Role:** HR Workflow.
   - **Process:** Structured form and tracking sequence for employees to initiate notice periods and separation from the institution. Syncs dynamically to the `admin-hr.html` portal.
