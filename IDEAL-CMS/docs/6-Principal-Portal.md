# 6. Principal / Director Portal Working Process

## Overview
The **Principal Portal** acts as the absolute ultimate viewer for the Academic execution in the institution. Rather than setting up logic (done by Admin) or teaching (done by Faculty), the Principal monitors overall operational health and sanctions structural changes.

## Working Process & Files involved

1. **`principal-dashboard.html`**
   - **Role:** High-level analytic observability.
   - **Process:** Acts as a command center summarizing all departments implicitly. Provides a quick 10,000-foot view of Top-performing cohorts, struggling departments, HR metrics, and an active context-switching drop-down for viewing specific branches.

2. **`principal-academic-info.html`**
   - **Role:** Detailed academic analytics.
   - **Process:** The Principal drills down into deep matrices. They assess:
     - The detailed Time Table structuring algorithm outputs across the college.
     - The Subject Distribution mappings to identify faculty workload imbalances.
     - Final Result declarations to audit pass/fail ratios across demographics before publicly releasing results.
