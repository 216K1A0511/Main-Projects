# 8. Parent / Guardian Portal Working Process

## Overview
The **Parent Portal** is a heavily isolated, read-only analytics view of their specific Ward's (Student) trajectory through the institution. It brings transparency to operations without allowing the parent to alter system states.

## Working Process & Files involved

1. **`parent-dashboard.html`**
   - **Role:** Unified observability.
   - **Process:** The parent inputs their Ward's ID and tracks:
     - **Academics:** Views precise attendance heatmaps implicitly tied to the Faculty's roll-call, and views uploaded grades for midterms and finals dynamically updated by HODs.
     - **Extracurricular & Fees:** Tracks outstanding institutional fee structures and follows the student's registration to physical events taking place in the college.
     - **Engagement:** Can interact via an internal email channel specifically mapped to interface with their Ward's designated instructors or the central Admin office.
